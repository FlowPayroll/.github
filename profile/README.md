2. **Receive Payments** — Your client can complete the payment through a `USD` or `EUR` bank transfer without needing to create an account or register on the platform.
3. **Withdraw Your Funds** — Transfer your available balance to a local bank account or withdraw in `USDT` or `USDC` to a compatible cryptocurrency wallet using the `TRC20`, `ERC20`, or `BEP20` network.

All withdrawal requests undergo a verification and compliance review before processing, typically taking a few hours. Withdrawals can be configured to initiate automatically when funds are received or remain pending until manually authorized for release.
